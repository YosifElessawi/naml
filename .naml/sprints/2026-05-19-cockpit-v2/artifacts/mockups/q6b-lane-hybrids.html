RECOMMENDATION
Hybrid 1 — gantt with a fat lane gutter + idle "READY" band + ghost slots. Each lane row has a 210px left gutter showing lane status pill, current slice id + title, live duration + context % meters, and "this sprint" stats. The track itself uses dashed/empty regions to show "I'm ready, waiting for work" — that signal becomes the visual headline whenever a lane is starved. Future slices are sketched as ghost blocks beyond the now-line, so you can preview what's about to land on which lane.

A couple of micro-decisions either way:
  • Ghost blocks past "now" — show the scheduler's plan ahead of time, or only render what's already started? My pick: show ghosts, dimmer when speculation gets less certain (depends_on chain depth).
  • Time axis scaling — fixed window (always last 30m + 6m of future), or auto-fit to sprint duration? Auto-fit reads better for short sprints; fixed window matches a cockpit "live tape".
  • Idle "READY" band — glow always, or only pulse when there are ready slices queued (i.e., "you actually have work I could do")? Pulse-when-queued tells you something actionable.
